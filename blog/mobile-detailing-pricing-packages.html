Mobile Detailing Pricing: Package Structure That Raises Ticket Size

Detailers who sell "a detail" compete on price. Detailers who sell three named packages plus a maintenance plan compete on value — and win the ticket-size war.

The three-tier structure

Essential (wash, wheels, interior vacuum), Signature (Essential + clay, wax, interior dressing), Premium (Signature + engine bay, headlight restoration, pet-hair treatment). Most buyers choose the middle tier when three are presented — price it as the one you want to sell.

Charge for travel honestly

A travel fee inside a defined radius (free) and a per-mile rate outside it keeps you from subsidizing distant jobs. Publish the radius; it also creates urgency ("we cover your area Tuesdays").

The maintenance-plan upsell

After a Premium detail, offer a monthly or bi-monthly maintenance wash at a locked rate. Recurring clients smooth your week and raise lifetime value without new marketing.

FAQ

How much should mobile detailing cost? US metros 2026: roughly $100–$150 essential, $180–$280 signature, $300+ premium for a standard sedan — adjust for vehicle size and condition.

Do I charge extra for pet hair? Yes — flat add-on, quoted before arrival. Pet hair is the classic margin-killer when unpriced.
