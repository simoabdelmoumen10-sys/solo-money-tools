Pressure Washing Pricing Guide: Driveways, Houses & Commercial (2026)

Pressure washing is a volume game with thin margins if you price per job by feel — and a strong margin business if you price per unit with minimums. The structure that works:

Price per unit, not per hour

Driveways price per square foot, houses per exterior square foot or by story count, gutters per linear foot, decks per square foot with wood-condition multipliers. Unit pricing makes quotes instant and defensible.

Set a job minimum

A $149–$199 minimum (market-dependent) keeps small driveways from becoming unprofitable drives across town. Say it on the phone before you quote details.

Build the upsell ladder

Driveway → house wash → gutters → windows → concrete sealing. The second service on the same visit carries near-zero travel cost, which is where the margin lives. Offer a package price for two or three services bundled.

Commercial is a different quote

Storefronts and HOAs buy on recurring contracts, not one-time cleans. Price monthly service with a contract rate and get the agreement in writing.

FAQ

What do pressure washers charge per square foot? Commonly $0.15–$0.35/sq ft for flatwork in US metros in 2026, more for two-story houses and delicate surfaces.

How do I quote a house wash? By exterior size and story count, with a minimum. Bundle the driveway to raise ticket size.
